How it works

Drawing links

  • Point at a task, milestone or external item: a circle shows on its start (left) and end (right) edge.
  • Drag a circle onto another item's start or end. End to start makes FS, start to start SS, end to end FF, start to end SF.
  • The new link opens in the editor, where you can set its lag or mark it blocked.

Milestones and externals

  • A milestone sits on the step line after its last input.
  • An external item sits where its expected date falls; what links to it waits until it's delivered.
  • Link a whole feature to make every task in it wait.
  • Drag a diamond or square sideways to pin it to another gate.

Releases

  • Put a feature or task in a release from its editor.
  • Show on map, on a release's page, highlights its work and what it waits on.

Timeline and Gantt

  • Drag a plan bar to move it, or drag an end to resize it.
  • Drag a milestone or external marker to change its date.

Dates

  • Steps are a sequence and carry no dates.
  • Give tasks a start-by and due date, and milestones, external items and releases a due date, to flag late starts, overdue work and anything waiting on work due later.
  • The status date in Plan settings sets what counts as today.
