FS: starts after the other finishes. SS: starts once the other has started. FF: can finish only after the other finishes. SF: can finish only after the other has started. Lag adds or removes whole steps. Milestones are gates on the line after their last input. External dependencies sit where their expected date falls, and anything linked to them waits until they are delivered; link a whole feature to make every ticket in it wait. A flag in a step header marks where a release's last task lands. Steps carry no dates. Give tasks a start-by and due date, and milestones, external items and releases a due date, to flag late starts, overdue work and anything waiting on work due later.
